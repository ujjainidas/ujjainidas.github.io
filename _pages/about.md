Hi! 👋 I am a recent graduate that studied Computer Science and Robotics at [UT Austin](https://www.utexas.edu) in the [Turing Scholars](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/turing-scholars-honors-program) honors program. Advised by [Dr. Amy Pavel](https://amypavel.com), I am working on supporting and augmenting physical experiences. 

My recent work makes complex movement learning (_i.e.,_ [dance](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2511.09658)) accessible for blind and low-vision (BLV) learners. I've also collaborated on [accessible video-based task assistance](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.00717) and [designing accessible household robot oversight](https://minahuh.com/Accessible_Robot_Oversight.pdf). My work has been published at [CHI](https://dl.acm.org/conference/chi) and [UIST](https://sigchi.org/events/uist-2025/). Please see the [Publications](https://ujjainidas.github.io/publications/) tab for more information!

I am broadly excited about enhancing learning and creativity through adaptive, physical systems. I use community-centered approaches such as [co-design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.09658) and user-informed [system development and evaluation](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.00717) to amplify the voices of people underserved by technology.

I have also completed three internships at [Apple](https://www.apple.com), where I worked on features for [Apple Intelligence](https://www.apple.com/apple-intelligence/) and [iOS UI/UX](https://www.idownloadblog.com/2025/06/19/use-3d-wallpaper-iphone/). I am currently a full-time engineer building camera software across features including [in-call Video Effects](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105117), [Spatial Personas](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/guide/apple-vision-pro/tana1ea03f18/visionos), [Continuity Camera](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102546), and [Dual Capture](https://www.igeeksblog.com/how-to-use-dual-capture-video-on-iphone-17/). 

Beyond research, I enjoy dancing. I was trained in Bharatnatyam as a child and I began learning Hip-hop in college. I also enjoy making art and have worked with various mediums for the past 10 years. 

I'm eager to connect with people about new research ideas, or just to chat! Please feel free to reach out at [ujjaini@utexas.edu](mailto:ujjaini@utexas.edu)!
